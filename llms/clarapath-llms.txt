# ClaraPath

> ClaraPath is a precision pathology solutions company providing advanced tissue processing tools and services to hospitals, veterinary labs, and healthcare professionals.

ClaraPath develops and delivers innovative pathology instruments and consumables — including the SectionStar and TrimStar product lines — alongside expert consulting services.

Their mission is to improve diagnostic accuracy and streamline lab workflows for pathologists worldwide.
